## Platform

web

## Users

People deciding who should do their makeup or teach them to do it. The site addresses them in the feminine ("clientas"). The site serves three groups, each with its own role in the business:

- **Class students:** want to learn to do their own makeup or deepen a technique. In person in Benalmádena, or online from anywhere Spanish is spoken.
- **Brides:** choosing the Artist for their wedding, a high-stakes decision. They request a Quote, then go through an Interview and a Trial.
- **Event guests and Brow Design clients:** need a Service for a specific occasion or on a regular basis, and want to book it quickly. They are local to the Costa del Sol.

## Product Purpose

The brochure site of Fernanda Gutiérrez (the Artist), a professional makeup artist with a Studio in Benalmádena, Málaga. It presents her Services and Classes and sends every Booking or enquiry to WhatsApp. Success is more qualified WhatsApp enquiries across the year, and above all more Classes.

The groups matter for different reasons, in this order of strategic priority:

1. **Classes are the growth bet and the differentiator.** They earn very well, and the site owner wants many more of them.
2. **Bridal Makeup earns the most per Service,** but it is seasonal and can't sustain the business all year.
3. **Social Makeup, Brow Design and other Services** are complementary, but relevant: they bring in income all year.

## Positioning

An artist who both does the work and teaches it, with a natural style and a close personal relationship with each client. The Artist herself works with the client from first contact to the end.

According to the site owner there are no comparable Classes in the area. This guides strategy and local SEO, but it hasn't been verified: don't publish it as a claim ("the only one in Málaga") without confirming it.

## Operating Context

- Visitors act through WhatsApp. Nothing is booked or paid on the site.
- A Booking is confirmed with a 50% Deposit, paid outside the site.
- Services are offered at the Studio or at the client's home. Classes are in person or online.
- Bridal demand depends on the season. Online Classes depend on neither the season nor the location.
- Reach: the Costa del Sol for in-person Services and Classes, and Spanish speakers anywhere for online Classes. The site is in Spanish only.

## Evidence on Hand

- More than 22 years of experience, as stated on the site. It is supporting evidence, not the lead argument.
- Reviews on Google: 5.0 from 75 Reviews (November 2026). The ones Google ranks as most relevant mention weddings, warmth and attentive listening.
- Portfolio photos in `public/` and the data in `src/data/portfolio.ts`.
- Brands the Artist works with, including Dior, Chanel, Charlotte Tilbury and Pat McGrath Labs (`src/data/brands.ts`). They add credibility but aren't a differentiator.
- There is no count of students, Class testimonials, press or awards. Don't invent them.

## Product Principles

1. **Each group gets what it needs from the site:** Classes must be easy to discover and understand, Brides need reasons to trust, and year-round clients need a fast way to book.
2. **Give Classes more weight than their share of today's business.** They are where the business wants to grow.
3. **Lead with the person.** The site should make clear who the Artist is, how she works and how she treats clients.
4. **Every path ends in a WhatsApp conversation.** The site opens it, with the context of what the visitor wants.
5. **Claim only what can be shown.** Real Reviews and real work carry the argument.
