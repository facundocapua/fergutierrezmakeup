# Home

Scope: the home page, plus the shared header and footer. Visitor mode: Persuade. Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

Audience and job: visitors from Instagram or TikTok, Google and recommendations, many on phones. They must understand that the Artist both does makeup and teaches it, and open WhatsApp on the right path. Above all, more Class enquiries.

Unresolved: the Artist must review the drafted notes on the hero and Class photos. The other pages keep their old layouts until they are revamped; branch `redesign` isn't merged until then.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A real face treated as the Artist's working face chart, flanked by her two verbs. It refuses the beauty-site hero of a portrait, a tagline and a row of service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool chart paper, graphite ink and red pencil marks; whole sections in pigment fields (lipstick red, blush, bronze) taken from the photographs. Square-cornered actions, ruled rows instead of cards, hand-pencilled notes with swatches. No gold, no pink gradient, no cream.

STORY: She does it and she teaches it. Classes come first, then Bridal Makeup step by step, then fast booking, her work, Reviews and who she is. Everything ends in WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The face fills the centre from top to bottom. "Te maquillo." sits large on its left and "Te enseño." on its right, on one line. Under each, a sentence and its action: book on WhatsApp, or go to Classes. Four pencil notes with swatches sit below, with leader lines drawn onto the eyes, lips, brows and skin. Hovering or focusing a note isolates its mark.
