# Fer Gutierrez MakeUp

Brochure site for Fernanda Gutiérrez, a professional makeup artist. It presents her Services and Classes and routes every booking or enquiry to WhatsApp. Nothing is sold or paid on the site.

Canonical terms are in English (the language of the code); the Spanish term shown to visitors is given in parentheses.

## Language

### Business

**Artist**:
Fernanda Gutiérrez, the single professional behind the site.
_Avoid_: Pro, Store, Salon, owner

**Studio** ("Estudio"):
The physical place where the Artist works and receives clients.
_Avoid_: Salon, Store, Shop

**Contact Details**:
The Studio's public email, phone, address, opening hours, map link and social network profiles.
_Avoid_: Salon info, store data

### Offering

**Service** ("Servicio"):
A makeup or brow job the Artist performs on a client, at the Studio or at the client's home. Current Services: Social Makeup, Bridal Makeup and Brow Design.
_Avoid_: Treatment, product

**Social Makeup** ("Maquillaje Social"):
A Service for guests at events (parties, christenings, birthdays).

**Bridal Makeup** ("Maquillaje de Novias"):
A Service for a bride on her wedding day, preceded by an Interview and a Trial. It is requested with a Quote, not booked directly.
_Avoid_: Wedding makeup

**Brow Design** ("Diseño de Cejas"):
A Service that shapes and designs the client's eyebrows.
_Avoid_: Eyebrows, eyebrowns

**Class** ("Clase"):
A lesson where the Artist teaches the client, in person or online. Current Classes: Self-Makeup Class and Refresher Class. A Class is not a Service.
_Avoid_: Course, curso, workshop

**Self-Makeup Class** ("Automaquillaje individual"):
A one-to-one Class where the client learns to do their own makeup.
_Avoid_: Self makeup course

**Refresher Class** ("Perfeccionamiento"):
A personalised Class where the client deepens a technique they choose.
_Avoid_: Advanced course, masterclass

### Engagement

**Booking** ("Turno"):
A reserved slot for a Service or Class, arranged over WhatsApp.
_Avoid_: Appointment, reservation, order

**Deposit** ("Seña"):
The 50% advance payment that confirms a Booking. It is paid outside the site.
_Avoid_: Down payment, prepayment

**Quote** ("Presupuesto"):
A price the Artist works out for a request instead of a fixed price. It is used for Bridal Makeup.
_Avoid_: Estimate, budget

### Showcase

**Portfolio**:
The gallery of the Artist's past work.
_Avoid_: Gallery, porfolio

**Review** ("Reseña"):
A client's public opinion of the Artist, taken from the Artist's Google Business profile.
_Avoid_: Testimonial

**Brand** ("Marca"):
A cosmetics brand the Artist works with, shown for credibility. The site does not sell Brands.
_Avoid_: Partner, supplier
